01   WHY THIS MATTERS

The Problem with Conventional Feed & Silage Testing

PROBLEM CONTEXT

Feed and silage quality is often judged through visual inspection or delayed laboratory testing, making rapid, affordable and consistent on-farm decisions difficult for dairy farmers.

👁️

Manual Assessment

Farmers may depend on colour, smell, texture and experience when judging feed and silage quality.

⏱️

Testing Delays

Laboratory testing can take time, delaying decisions about feeding, storage and spoilage.

💰

Cost & Access

Small and medium dairy farmers may not have convenient access to affordable rapid testing facilities.

⚠️

Quality Risk

Poor-quality feed or silage can contribute to feed wastage, animal-health concerns and production losses.

03   SYSTEM WORKFLOW

How the Proposed System Works

01

Sample Collection

The farmer collects a feed or silage sample for assessment.

02

Image & Sensor Acquisition

The system captures a sample image and collects available moisture, temperature and pH parameters.

03

AI-Based Quality Analysis

Image processing and feature extraction evaluate visible quality-related characteristics.

04

Quality Classification

The proposed model classifies the sample into predefined quality grades with a confidence score.

05

Instant Digital Report

The interface presents quality, key parameters, risk indicators and suggested actions.

06

Farmer Dashboard

Results can be stored for history, trends, alerts and farm-level monitoring.
