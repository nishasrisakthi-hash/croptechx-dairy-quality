The problem

Traditional feed and silage assessment can depend heavily on visual/manual inspection and laboratory testing, creating delays and uncertainty for farm-level decisions.

!

Manual assessment

Visual judgement can vary between people and may miss important quality differences.

⏱

Slow testing

Laboratory-oriented testing can take time and may be difficult to access for small and medium dairy farms.

₹

Cost & access

A practical on-farm approach can reduce dependence on repeated external testing for rapid screening.

Our proposed solution

A modular digital system combining image analysis, quality classification, farmer recommendations and instant reporting. The current website includes a demo simulation; a trained ML model can be connected later.

AI

AI image analysis

Analyse feed or silage images for visible quality-related features such as colour and texture.

✓

Rapid classification

Present an interpretable quality grade with a confidence score for the demo result.

▣

Digital decision support

Convert the analysis into recommendations, reports, history and trend views for farmers.
